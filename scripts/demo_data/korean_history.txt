Hankuk University
HIS 110 Understanding Korean History
Credits: 2

Course Description
A survey of Korean history from ancient kingdoms to the modern era.

Weekly Schedule
Week 1: Prehistoric Korea and Gojoseon
Week 2: The Three Kingdoms
Week 3: Unified Silla and Balhae
Week 4: Goryeo dynasty
Week 5: Early Joseon
Week 6: Late Joseon and reforms
Week 7: The opening of ports
Week 8: Colonial period and independence movements
Week 9: Liberation and division
Week 10: Korean War
Week 11: Industrialization
Week 12: Democratization
Week 13: Contemporary Korea

Assessment
Essay 50%, Final exam 50%
